Apt. E

https://soundcloud.com/apte/apt-e-at-the-lodge-2022

Apt E is perhaps the most elusive export of Seattle, Washington. The record label, DJ duo, and crate digging youtube channel is headed by a pair of selectors with as much love for slowed n’ throwed beats and obscure house bombs as for each other.

Boogalo Jones

https://laramarecords.bandcamp.com/album/everyone-is-a-winner

Boogaloo Jones is a Bangladeshi-Canadian DJ & producer, based in Montreal, Canada. Mixing a wide range of genres, he brings an eclectic energy to dancefloors and listening spaces alike.

A part of various collectives such as Jugaar, Fella's Q Tips, 140/160 & CURLS, he hosts events monthly in Montreal as well as playing regularly in clubs and bars like Sans Soleil, Barbossa, Datcha, Mal Necessaire & Salon Badin.

As an artist he got his start on The Director's EP "Outtakes", put out by Kookoo Records featuring with an old school rap verses on the track "Fella's Interlude". Following up the same year with a collaborative EP on La Rama Records titled "Everyone Is A Winner" featuring his production duo The Wet Steppahz & another local duo DAPASHU? This fall he will be releasing his first solo EP with Breakglass Records. Paying homage to the folk musicians of Bengal, known as Baul musicians. He uses this platform to boost representation to his Bangladeshi roots to blend South Asian folk & classical music with 303s, breakbeats and all kinds of electronic goodness.

Claire

https://soundcloud.com/selektsounds/selekt-sessions-008-claire

Deep inside Montreal's underground Claire concocts her unique sound: sensual, and playful, traversing time and space. Whether playing vinyl, digital, or live with analog machines, her style exudes a nomadic exoticism, creating a special kind of magic wherever she goes. In 2025 she launched Planète Secrète: a vinyl label and platform to serve as an outlet for her artistic expression. From MUTEK and Piknic Electronik to Tokyo and Club Der Visionaere, Claire’s music will move you.

Dapapa

https://soundcloud.com/dapapapete/rockwall-trail-mix-wav

Montreal-based Dapapa operates at the intersection of creative hub and dancefloor conduit. Driven by his passion for community and music, he spent the last couple of years diligently building a multi disciplinary production house: DASA, where he produces his own music, records, and hosts various local and international artists. The sonic journey with Dapapa is eclectic, incorporating elements of deep house, tech house, electro, ukg, bass, breaks, club, often with his own tracks, and those of his friends.

Destiny

https://soundcloud.com/timeis4clocks/destination-memory-ti4c-june

Making the intelligent unintelligible, reducing acts of sonic alchemy to animal instinct & contorting the familiar into something alien are Destiny’s raisons d’etre. Grown in the 80,000+ strong vinyl archive of Etobicoke’s Association of Professional Record Players (her late grandfather’s DJ organization), she was born a legacy DJ, but her sound is nothing if not iconoclastic. Finding throughlines in all the wrong places, she reminds us that any & all music worth its salt is good for dancing - are you?

dj hi chew

dj hi chew is a Vancouver-born, Montreal-based DJ playing genre bending sets that will make you dance. Their selections will keep you on your toes in more ways than one.

DJ Spirit

https://soundcloud.com/xxdeejayspiritxx/dj-spirit-4-7am-set-flip-parquette-dec-14-2025

dj spirit is a dj and event organizer from montreal. They play digital sizzling techno and house. Stretched out songs that get blended and looped, constructions of futuristic drum-music and bouncing patterns. Glitched, crushed, steady music.

Eccina

https://audiobambino.bandcamp.com/album/flex-metallurgy

Eccina is a microorganism enthusiast and raver in Montreal. Find their music in the levitation chamber, rotating freely in all directions. It is acid-informed, buoyant, and body-ready. An improvised hardware live set has newly emerged from their latest research in psychotropic sonic propulsion and state-of-the-art groove sensibilities. On the dance floor, Eccina is inspired by hypnotic rhythms, preposterous timbres, and fun.

Emma

Emma is a Montreal based DJ exploring the cross sections of tender, swamp, and celebration. A chunky dancefloor where you can feel safe sharing your secrets.

Figue

https://soundcloud.com/clydebuiltradio/figue-5

Figue is a Montreal-based radio show host and dj who draws inspiration from an eclectic array of genres (dance and non-dance music alike) and is interested in weaving stories with her sets.

Icky Magdala

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W7Ky2aG_-Mk

Icky Magdala is a Montreal-based DJ and producer with a career spanning nearly two decades. Originating from the city’s underground scene, she finds refuge in sound system culture. Drawing upon a deep affection for different subgenres—with heavy emphasis on bass and drum lines—the avid vinyl records collector navigates through dub, jungle, drum & bass, hip hop, and acid techno. This has led her to collaborate with notable music institutions such as Piknic Electronik, Mutek, The Lot Radio in NYC, and numerous encounters with the Dub-Stuy crew, to name a few. When she isn’t DJing, playing live sets or curating events, Icky Magdala continues to expand her sonic vision with her project Bridges to Dub. Her mission: spreading the music and good vibes.

Iris

https://soundcloud.com/underground-love-affair/ula-021-iris

Iris is a Montreal-based DJ, producer, multidisciplinary artist and founder of Underground Love Affair, a mix & party series spotlighting Queer DJ talent.

As a sonic contributor to Montréal's Queer underground his playing style mixes an insatiable appetite for music of all kinds guided with a strong, emotional sense of knowing.

Kiju

https://soundcloud.com/weirdos-inc/kiju-montreal-incternational-mix-series-08-weirdos-inc?in=kijulia/sets/mixes

Kiju is a celebrated organizer and versatile DJ with equal parts range and focus, rewarding listeners with new and unimagined ways of returning to the body.

Liuai

https://soundcloud.com/weewayway/cave-of-dreams-live

Liuai is a multi-instrumentalist, originally from Montréal and currently residing in San Francisco. Primarily a flautist, she splits her time between performing in classical chamber music ensembles and electronic music production. Her recent projects include sample-based generative music and sonic worldbuilding through narratives, flute, and textural elements. Her current project, Mire & Meadow, will be played at Murmur and is inspired by explorations of her family’s ties with marshes and the landscape’s ability to invoke feelings of discovery and letting go.

Malte Leander

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bHsIsCFQ9UA

Malte Leander is a Swedish-French multidisciplinary artist currently residing in Tiohtià:ke/Montreal, Canada. Primarily working in sound and performance, his sonic explorations and artistic research often revolve around various natural environments, from urban spaces to coastlines and ocean ecology. He seeks to engage audiences through multisensory exploration around the topics of non-anthropocentric listening, deep listening practices, soundwalks and soundscape composition.

In this approach around ecologies, Malte works in both live performance and installation, exploring shifts of perspectives when interactions and overlaps between the human and the non-human occur, in order to challenge the understanding of timescales and hierarchies.

Miette

https://soundcloud.com/thetyss/dewpoint

Miette builds her sets around minimalist ethics and experimental sensibilities. She believes in repetition as a driver of trance and movement and is fascinated by genre agnostic areas of music, places where categories blur, bend and blend. Her sound is formally rooted in psychedelic techno, but she also draws heavily on dub, minimal, bass and electro.

MPM10

https://soundcloud.com/mpm10/ketum-closing

MPM10 AKA Michele Azur is a community organizer in Montreal’s DIY arts and music scene. Under STMUnderground and other monikers he hosts ambient and dance music events in clandestine locations around the city. An avid listener of sounds from the UK and beyond, you can catch his show The Tunnel on CKUT 90.3FM on alternating Mondays at midnight.

Mx. Blaire

https://soundcloud.com/smut-press/075-mx-blaire

A dazzling selectress, an embodiment of skill and craft, and a passionate community builder, Mx. Blaire (she/they) has a singular ability to enchant dancers with stirring, sometimes otherworldly sounds, grounded by strong basslines and beats.

Blaire’s reverence for 90s house, hard-hitting techno, and mind-bending acid guarantees an active dance floor, kept enthralled by intentionally placed breaks and thrilling interplays of tempos and textures. As a result of their dedicated vinyl practice, Blaire’s sets are also technically engaging, with brilliant phrasing and thoughtful touches of turntablism. Her mastery behind the decks gives Blaire the space to really work her selections—manipulating the record unites her energy with the track and transmits it to every dancer on the floor. Through these sonic communications, they explore concurrent themes of resistance & joy and existence & play with the crowd.

In addition to sharing her gifts through her DJ sets, Blaire betters Boston’s underground through her organizing, for the queer dance music collective Boudoir and her new series Sheer Bliss. These themed parties—graced by guests like Ariel Zetina, Lakuti, and SPRKLBB and Mutualism—exhibit the extraordinary strength and kinship of the community Blaire has helped come together. It’s hard to find parties filled with as much uninhibited delight. Furthering her commitment to community care, she has created a space for rejuvenation of body and mind during turbulent times, her intimate Sunday ambient and downtempo party called Sweetie Pie.

A trusted and beloved star in Boston, Blaire has caught the attention of formidable East Coast and Midwest DJs and organizers from Nick Boyd/Sorry Records in New York to Club Chow/Club Gauche in Chicago to Jellyfish in Pittsburgh, and they have played such clubs as Good Room, Basement, smartbar, and Marble Bar. In fall 2024, she went on the road with Eris Drew and Octo Octa for their LUV NRG Soundsystem tour—a perfect fit, as Blaire exhibits the sincerity, the deftness, and the rave-rousing multidimensionality the pair is famous for and that will keep her captivating dancers for many years to come.

Along with driving the queer scene in Boston - Blaire is a curator of annual rave in Vermont - System Activate - alongside Octo Octa & Eris Drew - bringing the 42,000 Watt T4T LUV NRG sound system to a gorgeous forest clearing for a weekend of psychedelic music & retreat.

Trauma Response Unit

https://traumaresponseunit.bandcamp.com/album/the-reason-why-i

Trauma Response Unit is a live experimental music project rooted in looking within oneself ~ shadow work, grief, and lamenting the turmoil we carry. Moving through drone, noise, dub, industrial electronics and rhythm, each set is less a performance with a fixed destination than a process of working through what is there in the moment. Sounds build, fracture, repeat and transform; heaviness can become movement, and grief can become something physical. At its core, Trauma Response Unit is about sitting with what hurts rather than turning away from it. Lamenting the turmoil, looking into the shadow, and finding the thing that remains underneath it all ~ I’m still here, despite it all ~
